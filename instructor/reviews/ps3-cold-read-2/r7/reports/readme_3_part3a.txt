README.md:B047: FVA finds the min and max of each flux over all optimal solutions, or over all solutions with production at least a fraction γ of the optimum. It cites Mahadevan and Schilling (2003) as the source.
README.md:B048: γ is the `fraction` argument of supply_ranges and lies in [0,1]. The default of 1 keeps only optimal solutions; 0.99 allows a 1% drop in production.
README.md:B049: FVA is applied only to the amino-acid supply fluxes. Internal forward/reverse column pairs give uninformative ranges, and supply reactions have no reverse partner.
README.md:B050: Defines c (protein objective) and z⋆ (maximum production in mM/h for one strategy and budget), and introduces a range for each supply column.
README.md:B051: Each supply flux's min and max are computed subject to steady state, the bounds, A v ≤ b, and production ≥ γ z⋆.
README.md:B052: The bounds, A and b in the FVA problems are the ones allocation_constraints returns for that strategy and budget.
README.md:B053: Defines a "fixed" supply flux as one with range width ≤ 1e-5 mM/h. The driver and questions 3–5 use this rule, and supply_ranges returns only the endpoints.
