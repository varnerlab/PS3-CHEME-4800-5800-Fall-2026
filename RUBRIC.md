# PS3 grading rubric

PS3 is worth a maximum of **4 points**. There are **72 tests**, 24 in each of
[testme_part_1.jl](testme_part_1.jl), [testme_part_2.jl](testme_part_2.jl), and
[testme_part_3.jl](testme_part_3.jl).

| Score | Requirements |
|:---:|---|
| 0 | Nothing runs; no tests pass. |
| 1 | Some tests pass, but not a majority (1–36). |
| 2 | A majority pass, but at least one test fails (37–71). |
| 3 | All 72 tests pass, but the questions in `responses.md` are not answered. |
| 4 | All 72 tests pass and the questions in `responses.md` are answered. |

To grade, the instructor copies your `src/Compute.jl`, any helper files, and
`Include.jl` into a clean copy of the assignment, reruns the tests, and then reads
your `responses.md`.

The policies in [README.md](README.md) also apply: no initial submission, or
copying the reference solution, scores `0`.
